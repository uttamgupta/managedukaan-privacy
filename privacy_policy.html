Privacy Policy for ManageDukaan

Effective date: 6 September 2026  ·  Last updated: 6 September 2026
Applies to the Android application com.managedukaan.app

In short: ManageDukaan is a cloud-synced app. Your shop data is stored on your device and also on Google Firebase servers so it syncs across your devices and your shop staff. We do not sell your data, we do not show ads, and we do not use advertising identifiers. We do use Google's standard app analytics to understand app stability and usage.

1. Who we are

ManageDukaan ("we", "our", "us") provides a point-of-sale, inventory and credit-ledger (khata) application for small retail shops. This policy explains what information the app handles, why, and what choices you have. It applies to the ManageDukaan Android app and the backend services that support it.

2. Information we handle

2.1 Account information

You sign in with your mobile number. We collect and store:

  • Your phone number (used as your login identity, verified by one-time SMS code)
  • Your display name, your role (owner, manager, cashier or viewer), the shops you belong to, and account creation / last sign-in timestamps

Phone verification is performed by Google Firebase Authentication. Delivery of the one-time code involves your mobile network operator.

2.2 Shop and business data

Data you enter while running your shop, including: shop name and owner contact number, product catalogue and prices, stock and purchase records, supplier details, sales transactions, payment records, and credit (khata) balances.

2.3 Customer and staff details you enter

To run a credit ledger you may record a customer's name, an optional phone number, and free-text notes. To share a shop you may add staff members by name, phone number and role.

Your responsibility: these are other people's personal details. By entering them you confirm you have a lawful basis to do so, and you are responsible for how you use them — for example when sending a payment reminder. We process this information on your behalf.

2.4 Contacts

If you tap "From Contacts" when adding a customer, the app asks for contacts permission and opens your phone's system contact picker. Only the single contact you choose is read, and only that contact's name and phone number. The app does not read, upload or index your contact list in bulk. If you decline the permission, you can still add customers by typing their details.

2.5 Camera and photos

  • Barcode scanning — the camera reads barcodes on-device. Camera frames are not recorded or uploaded.
  • Purchase invoice photos and shop logo — images you choose to attach are uploaded to Google Firebase Storage under your shop's account so they sync across devices.
  • Receipt scanning (AI) — if you use the receipt-scan feature, the receipt image is sent to Google's Gemini model through Firebase AI Logic to extract line items. See section 4.

2.6 Analytics and diagnostics

The app includes Google Firebase Analytics, which automatically collects standard app events (such as first open, session and screen activity), basic device and app-version information, and a Firebase app instance identifier. This is used to understand stability and feature usage in aggregate.

We do not use the Android Advertising ID. The AD_ID permission is explicitly removed from the app and advertising-ID collection is disabled in the app's configuration. We serve no advertising.

2.7 Bluetooth and location permissions

The app requests Bluetooth permissions to connect to thermal receipt printers and barcode scanners. On older Android versions the operating system requires a location permission in order to scan for nearby Bluetooth devices. We do not collect, use, store or infer your location.

3. Where your data is stored

ManageDukaan is offline-first: your data is written to local storage on your device so the app works without a network connection. The same data is synchronised to Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore, Firebase Storage and Firebase Authentication), operated by Google LLC, so that it is backed up and available on your other devices and to staff you have added to your shop. Data in transit is encrypted using HTTPS/TLS, and is encrypted at rest by Google Cloud. Access is restricted by security rules to your own shop account.

This means your data may be stored and processed on servers outside your country.

5. Why we process your data

  • To provide the app — billing, inventory, khata, receipts and invoices
  • To sync and back up your data across devices and shop staff
  • To authenticate you and control who can access your shop
  • To keep the app working — diagnosing crashes and understanding aggregate usage

We do not use your shop's business records to build advertising profiles.

6. How long we keep data

We keep your data for as long as your account is active, so your records remain available to you. Deleted records are retained briefly as deletion markers so the deletion propagates to your other devices, and are then purged. If you ask us to delete your account, see section 7.

7. Your rights and how to delete your data

Within the app you can view, edit, export (CSV / backup file) and delete individual records at any time.

To delete your account and all associated data, email support@managedukaan.com from your registered address, or include your registered mobile number, with the subject "Delete my ManageDukaan account". We will verify the request and delete your account, your shop records, and any uploaded images. We aim to complete this within 30 days.

You may also request a copy of your data, correction of inaccurate data, or withdrawal of consent (by discontinuing use and requesting deletion) using the same address.

Uninstalling the app removes the local copy from your device but does not delete data already synced to the cloud — use the deletion request above for that.

8. Children

ManageDukaan is a business tool intended for shop owners and their staff. It is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

9. Security

We rely on Google Firebase's encryption in transit and at rest, and on server-side security rules that restrict each shop's data to its authorised members. No system is perfectly secure; please keep your device and your sign-in number secure, and remove staff members who should no longer have access.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the app changes. The effective date at the top will be updated, and material changes will be reflected here before they take effect.

11. Contact and grievances

For any privacy question, data request or grievance: support@managedukaan.com. We aim to respond within 7 days, in line with applicable Indian grievance-redressal expectations.

This policy is intended to meet the requirements of Google Play's User Data and Data safety policies, India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023, and, where applicable, the GDPR and CCPA.
